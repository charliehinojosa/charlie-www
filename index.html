Everyone has the tools. Taste is the job.

In 1999 I was a webmaster . Knowing the tools was rare. Now AI hands everyone the tools , and the internet is filling with slop. What's scarce is taste.

Creativity is subjective. Taste is a point of view you can be held to: knowing what's good, what's off, and what to cut. Twenty-seven years across tech, music, film, brand and startups built mine. I know the tools, so I can make almost anything, or take what you've made and turn it into a work of art.
